# Proposed delivery, returns, and warranty policy

Draft for BUZIMA BOOSTER GROUP to review before publishing. These proposed terms are not yet customer commitments. Confirm delivery capacity, supplier warranty coverage, and refund handling before adopting them. Prices and terms communicated to a customer at purchase should be retained with their order.

## Delivery

- Delivery in Kigali: estimated 1–2 business days after order confirmation and payment confirmation where advance payment is required.
- Other serviceable locations in Rwanda: estimated 3–5 business days. Confirm availability and any special arrangements before accepting the order.
- Business days: Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays.
- Delivery estimates are not guarantees. If a delay occurs, contact the customer with an updated estimate and available options.
- Delivery charges: show the shipping fee in the checkout order summary before the customer places an order. Use the existing configured shipping charge initially; introduce location-based rates only when checkout supports them.
- Free delivery: offer only where checkout explicitly applies a qualifying promotion. Define the promotion threshold and covered locations before advertising it.
- Customers should provide a complete address, landmark, and reachable telephone number. Address changes must be requested before dispatch and confirmed by support.
- If delivery cannot be completed, contact the customer to arrange another attempt. Disclose any additional delivery fee and obtain agreement before charging it.
- Ask customers to check their package and items on arrival. Encourage reporting missing, incorrect, or visibly damaged items within 48 hours with photos and an order number. Later reports should still be assessed; this reporting request is not an automatic rejection deadline.

## Returns and exchanges

- Proposed change-of-mind window: request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery.
- Change-of-mind items should be unused, complete, and in their original packaging, with proof of purchase. Opened hygiene-sensitive items such as in-ear earphones, activated software, and digital codes are not eligible for change-of-mind returns. Explain any product-specific restriction before purchase.
- Incorrect, damaged, or faulty products are handled separately from change-of-mind returns. Ask customers to report problems promptly and assess each case, including issues discovered after the initial return window.
- Contact support before sending a return. Support provides the return location and instructions. Arrange return handover within 7 calendar days after instructions are issued, or agree on an extension.
- Store error, arrival damage, or a confirmed fault: the store covers reasonable agreed return transport costs. Change of mind: the customer covers agreed return transport costs.
- Inspect the returned item and communicate the outcome within 3 business days of receipt where possible. If assessment needs longer, provide an updated estimate.
- Offer a replacement, exchange, repair, or refund as appropriate to the issue and applicable purchase terms. Do not require a customer to accept store credit in place of an agreed refund.
- Initiate approved refunds within 5 business days through the original payment method where available, or another method agreed with the customer. Payment-provider processing may add time. Provide confirmation when the refund is initiated.
- Customers should back up data and remove personal accounts before handing over a device, following support instructions. Do not request account passwords.

## Warranty

- Coverage is product-specific. Publish a warranty duration and provider for each product only after confirming them with the supplier or manufacturer.
- Proposed target for new devices: 12 months of coverage for manufacturing defects, only where the store can support that commitment or the supplier confirms it. Until then, use the confirmed duration supplied with the product rather than a blanket promise.
- Accessories, batteries, refurbished goods, and used devices may have different coverage. State their confirmed terms before purchase.
- Retain proof of purchase and, where applicable, the model and serial number. An order record can help verify a purchase if the customer no longer has the receipt.
- Ask customers to describe the fault, when it began, and any troubleshooting attempted. Support will arrange assessment and explain next steps.
- Typical manufacturer exclusions may include accidental or liquid damage, ordinary wear, and damage caused by misuse or unauthorized modification. Apply only the actual confirmed warranty terms and explain any rejection with the assessment findings.
- Aim to provide an initial assessment update within 3 business days after receiving the device. Repair completion depends on diagnosis, parts, and the service provider; communicate an estimate after assessment and update the customer when it changes.
- Repairs or replacements should be arranged without charge for a confirmed covered fault, including agreed service transport. Obtain customer approval for any quoted work outside coverage before proceeding.
- Ask customers to back up data before service. Explain whether a reset may be required; never promise data recovery.
